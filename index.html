What the receipt tells you

The SHA-256 digest identifies the file bytes. A confirmed memo records that digest with a signing wallet on Solana devnet.

What it cannot tell you

It does not establish authorship, originality, ownership or a teacher’s acceptance. Devnet is a test network. Keep the file and receipt somewhere safe.
